## What it does (approved design 7e1ef4c1…, with your §11 choices)

**Budgets and slots**
- Budgets: control 25/50/99 epochs, Atlantic 13/26/52, from N5's step rule. Seeds 10–29.
- Slots: the control has 5 H9 checkpoints, the Atlantic arm 4.

**Selection, from recorded validation values only**
- the native pick at 1×, 2× and 4× (cross-checked against train.py's own pick, with the tie rule);
- matched odd grid at 4× (control);
- smoothed at 4×.

**H9 reads**
- Only the fixed slot epochs are read.
- An H9 file for any other epoch is refused, as are 2025, East Pacific or validation storms.

**Primaries: P1 (control) and P2 (Atlantic), 4× − 1×**
- Exact sign-flip test, α 0.025 each, SESOI 0.9 kt, N5's verdict wording.
- Each row states n, the number of non-zero seeds m and the p floor. The verdict says "significance unreachable" when the floor is at or above α.

**Secondaries and reports**
- S1/S2 (Atlantic − control) **only at 4×**, printed beside N5's verdict lines, quoted exactly from N5's pinned report.
- No Atlantic − control test at 1× or 2× anywhere; a test enforces this.
- BUDGET-LIMITED per arm per budget.
- Descriptive outputs: the budget curve, the per-storm table, the 10- and 20-member ensembles, and the frozen ensemble's committed figures (unpaired).

**Disk and guards**
- Only selected and H9-evaluated checkpoints are kept. Every deleted epoch's hash is recorded under its own key.
- train.py must equal the pinned afceb4b1… (your learning-rate condition).

**Your rule (A), as in N5**
