## hc-adversary item 16: CONCERN (high)

**Finding 1 (high).** Condition (b) holds inside one launch. The weak point is a relaunch from a different commit, which is the most likely restart, because a crash in the launch script needs a fix PR.
- Such a relaunch passes every guard and runs all 30 H9 evaluations.
- Only then does the analysis refuse ("runs come from more than one commit").
- That would force a choice after looking at held-out results, which is what (b) exists to prevent.
