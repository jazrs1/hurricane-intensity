## The design in brief

- **A new experiment, not a rerun or rescue of N5.**
  - N5's verdict stands whatever F-31 finds.
  - No N5 run, checkpoint, CSV or seed is reused, and nothing is pooled.
  - N5's files are read-only inputs.
- **Two training arms.** The N5 control (whose recipe is exactly the frozen ensemble's) and the N5 Atlantic arm.
  - The frozen ensemble enters as descriptive 10- and 20-member ensembles built from the control runs.
  - The frozen members themselves are never re-evaluated.
  - hc-adversary confirmed a separate ensemble arm would only duplicate the control.
- **Nested budgets.** Each run trains once to 4× the N5 budget: control up to 99 epochs, Atlantic up to 52.
  - It is read at 1×, 2× and 4× (1650/3300/6534 steps for the control; 1651/3302/6604 for Atlantic).
  - This is exactly equivalent to separate runs: train.py's learning rate is constant and its loop doesn't depend on the epoch count.
- **Selection on validation only.**
  - At every budget: the native pick, with the N5 tie rule.
  - At 4× only: the matched odd-epoch grid, and a smoothed 5-epoch-median rule as a sensitivity.
  - H9 is evaluated only at these pre-fixed checkpoints: at most 180 evaluations in all, and no per-epoch H9 trace.
- **Primaries.** P1 (control) and P2 (Atlantic): H9 overall MAE at 4× minus 1×, paired within each run.
  - Exact sign-flip test, α 0.025 each (Bonferroni), SESOI 0.9 kt (N5's, for comparability).
- **Secondary.** Atlantic minus control at 4× (S1 MAE, S2 >96 kt bias), printed beside N5's verdict quoted exactly.
  - **No Atlantic-minus-control test at 1× or 2×.** That would be N5's question asked again.
- **Seeds and power.**
  - Seeds 10–29: 20 per arm, none reused from N5.
  - At the sd N5 actually observed (1.12 kt), there is 87–100% power to detect 0.9 kt; plan on 87%.
  - Stated limit: the test can only reach significance if at least 7 of 20 seeds pick a later epoch at 4× than at 1×. The power figures assume that happens, so they are optimistic by however many don't. That count is known from validation before any H9 look.
- **BUDGET-LIMITED.** N5's rule, applied per arm per budget; the qualifier fires at ≥ 10/20 flagged runs. All flags are known before the H9 look. F-31 doesn't extend itself if 4× still fires.
- **Compute.**
  - Phase 1 (training) about 5.8 h (5.5–7 h).
  - Phase 2 (H9) about 20 min.
  - About 10 GB kept on disk.
- **Paths.** Only `models/f31`, `results/f31` and `logs/f31` are written. N5's folders, `models/storms`, `results/ensemble`, `results/final_2025`, the 14 protected files, `data/` and H-EP are never written.
- **Launch safety.** N5's machinery carries over unchanged; §8 lists the rows that differ.

## Evaluation set (§4): H9, reused, and what that means

H9 is the only usable set:
- no unspent Atlantic storm with frames above 96 kt remains;
- validation is the selector;
- 2025 is spent;
- H-EP is sealed under its own protocol.

The design states plainly what reusing H9 means:
- absolute levels say nothing about new storms;
- every pre-registered quantity is a difference between validation-selected checkpoints;
- inference is over seeds, on these 9 storms;
- F-31 was partly prompted by N5's H9 outcome, which is why Atlantic minus control is secondary, not primary.

A model trained longer that looks better here would still need its own test on an unspent set. That is option D, a future 2026-season set under its own protocol, not proposed here.

## The six open choices (§11), with recommendations

1. **Seeds: 20 per arm (recommended) or 10.** 10 halves training to about 2.9 h, but power at the observed sd falls from 87% to about 45%.
2. **Primaries: two within-arm budget effects (recommended), or three** (adding Atlantic minus control at 4× as P3, each at α 0.0167). Making the N5 contrast primary on the same H9, right after N5's look, reads as a second attempt at N5's question.
3. **Epoch sensitivity: validation-only plus the per-budget and per-rule H9 slots (recommended).** The alternative, H9 at ±1 epoch around each pick, would be a held-out per-epoch trace. Your 2026-10-18 rule forbids that.
4. **Disk: keep only the selected epoch files, about 10 GB (recommended), or all of them until the results entry, about 137 GB.**
5. **Evaluation set: H9, as above (recommended; the only usable set).**
6. **Matched slots at 1× and 2×: dropped (recommended).** Restoring them adds up to 40 H9 evaluations. They would stay descriptive.
