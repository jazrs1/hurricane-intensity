## What else the design fixes

- **E3 = v2's recipe plus East Pacific training storms**, seeds 40–49. No high-end handling: N5's and F-31's >96 kt results were all null.
- **East Pacific training storms are picked by a mechanical rule**, applied later by a reviewed script:
  - **Floors:** minimum peak intensity and minimum frame count, both set from the 19 Atlantic training storms.
  - **Viewing angle:** frames above the Atlantic train+val maximum angle are dropped.
  - **Order:** storms are added in SHA-256-of-ID order, up to the frame target.
- **Concurrency gate (hard):** any training frame whose crop box overlaps an H-EP or concurrency-excluded storm's box at the same time is dropped. Positions only. The launch re-checks it.
- **Preprocessing:** fc71b98 can't build East Pacific crops, so the H-EP pass must prove byte-identical crops for every storm scored. E3's code pins are taken after your antimeridian fix merges.
- **Freeze before H-EP**, like v2. E3's deadline is about end-January 2027.
