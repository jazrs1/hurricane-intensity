# PR #46: select_e3_ep.py, E3's East Pacific training-storm selection (§9.3)

**Needs John: one choice, the minimum frame count per EP storm.** The merge request comes after the change and the full check.

## The choice

The design sets the floor as "≥ the minimum per-storm frame count over the same 19 Atlantic train storms". That minimum is 25 frames, from AL012024. AL012024 is a control storm built hourly, so its 25 frames cover about 25 h. EP storms are built at 3h, where 25 frames means about 75 h, a floor three times stricter in duration than the storm that set it.

| Option | Floor | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | 25 frames, about 75 h | The design's letter; the script does this now |
| (b) | 36 frames, about 108 h | Minimum over the 10 Atlantic storms built at 3h (AL092024) |
| **(c) recommended** | **9 frames, about 25 h** | The same 19 storms, matched in duration |

**Why (c):**
- The floor exists to keep out short-lived storms, which is a matter of duration. (c) keeps the design's set and its shortest storm, measured in hours rather than in frames at a different cadence.
- GOES-18 for 2023–24 already limits how many EP storms qualify, so a stricter floor makes it more likely that E3 falls short of the 1963-frame target.
- (b) is the cleanest frame-for-frame match, but it uses a subset of the training storms, not all 19.
