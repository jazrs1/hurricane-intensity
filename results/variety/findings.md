# Variety-against-volume experiment findings

Generated by `scripts/variety_analysis.py`, which was written, smoke-tested on
synthetic data and committed before launch (`04b81f2`). Its SHA-256,
`2ba4d247...51fe17a5`, is recorded in `logs/decisions.md` and in
`logs/variety/analysis_sha256_at_launch.txt`, and still matches the file
after the run; the script is unchanged since the pre-registration commit.
The 20 training frame lists in `frame_lists/` (one per arm and seed, written
by `scripts/variety_subsets.py`) match their hashes at launch
(`logs/variety/frame_lists_sha256_at_launch.txt`). Inputs: the per-sample
prediction CSVs in this folder (`<arm>_seed<s>_h9.csv`), the per-run
storm-fraction files (`<arm>_seed<s>_storm_fraction_h9.json`) and the
training logs in `logs/variety/`. Per-run numbers in `summary_per_run.csv`,
per-arm tables in `summary_by_arm.csv`, the three primary tests in
`primary_tests.csv`, the robustness check in `robustness.csv`, everything
else in `exploratory_differences.csv`. Runs made by
`scripts/variety_experiment.sh`. Design, final design and results entries
in `logs/decisions.md` (2026-10-14). Only each run's validation-selected
checkpoint was kept (`models/variety/<arm>/seed<s>.pt`). `best_model.pt` and
`models/baseline_9kt.pt` were not touched.

## Result: variety helps bias and overall error; ranking not established

At an identical frame count and an identical intensity mix, training on 19
storms sampled 3-hourly beat training on 9 storms sampled hourly on two of
the three pre-registered tests: the high-end underestimation shrank and
overall error fell on the nine held-out storms. The high-end ranking test
did not survive correction.

| test (H9 pooled, sparse - dense) | dense (9 storms) | sparse (19 storms) | difference | exact p | survives |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spearman, frames > 96 kt | 0.532 (0.024) | 0.564 (0.040) | +0.031 | 0.047 | no |
| >96 kt bias | -11.14 (2.64) | -6.88 (2.34) | +4.26 | 0.0017 | yes |
| overall MAE | 10.75 (0.65) | 9.55 (0.39) | -1.20 | 0.00016 | yes |

Mean (sd) over ten runs. Two-sided exact permutation test on the difference
of arm means (all 184,756 splits), Bonferroni threshold 0.05 / 3 = 0.0167.
(The results entry in `logs/decisions.md` gives the MAE difference as
-1.21, a rounding of the printed -1.205; the unrounded value is -1.2046.)

Of the three readings fixed in advance, the first applies: "sparse beats
dense on a primary: at fixed volume and intensity mix, variety helps". It
applies on >96 kt bias and overall MAE. Dense did not beat sparse on
anything.

The Spearman difference, +0.031, is below what this design could resolve
(about 0.05 at 80% power), so the miss says only that a ranking effect of
this size is not established, not that there is none. The
more-distinct-storms experiment's ranking gain (+0.057) also failed its
robustness check.

## Volume and intensity mix were held fixed

The more-distinct-storms experiment could not separate variety from volume:
its expanded arm had more storms, more frames and more high-end frames at
once. Here only the number of storms (and with it, sampling density)
differs:

| | dense | sparse |
|---|---|---|
| distinct training storms | 9 (hourly) | 19 (3-hourly) |
| training frames | 1013 | 1013 |
| frames >= 96 / >= 113 / >= 137 kt | 178 / 106 / 26 | 178 / 106 / 26 |
| largest single storm | 28.4% (Dorian) | 11.5% (Florence) |
| epochs x batches | 51 x 32 = 1632 steps | 51 x 32 = 1632 steps |

Frames are matched count for count in each 5 kt bin, nested in the
Saffir-Simpson bands, and drawn fresh for every seed. One residual mismatch
remains: the sparse arm's training labels sit 0.3-0.9 kt lower within every
band, because 3-hourly best-track labels fall more often on bin floors
than hourly interpolated ones. Lower training labels would pull the sparse
arm's predictions down, the opposite of the observed +4.3 kt bias gain, so
the mismatch cannot explain the result.

Cross-experiment, descriptive only (the frame counts, step budgets and
control arms differ, so this is not a test): going from 9 to 19 storms
with more frames gave +5.40 kt bias and -1.60 kt MAE; with frames held
equal it gives +4.26 and -1.20, about four-fifths and three-quarters
respectively. Variety alone accounts for most of the earlier gain.

## Pre-registered secondary: null

The same-storm neighbour fraction on the H9 frames (k = 20, same-storm
frames within +-6 h excluded; neither arm trains on H9, so chance is
identical) went from 0.336 to 0.314 (-0.022, exact p 0.30). The direction
is the one predicted, but it is not significant. This is the third
experiment in which the storm-identity mechanism has not shown up in the
embedding. Whatever the extra storms change, this measure does not
detect it.

## Robustness check, fixed in advance

Each arm's seeds 0-4 against the other arm's ten runs, same test, 0.0167
(exact permutation over 3003 splits):

| metric | dense seeds 0-4 vs sparse | p | sparse seeds 0-4 vs dense | p | passes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spearman, frames > 96 kt | +0.033 | 0.13 | +0.025 | 0.23 | **no** |
| >96 kt bias | +3.82 | 0.009 | +5.11 | 0.003 | yes |
| overall MAE | -0.99 | 0.0003 | -1.27 | 0.003 | yes |

Bias and overall MAE hold in both directions; Spearman fails in both.

## Flags set in advance

**Under-training: not flagged.** Epoch 50 or 51 of 51 was selected by 1 of
10 dense and 3 of 10 sparse runs (dense 36-50, sparse 34-51).

**Run order.** H9 overall MAE against seed, within arm: dense +0.42, sparse
+0.02. Pooled against run order with arm means removed, +0.27 (p 0.25).
The first run of each round was 0.52 kt better than the second (exact
p 0.021). The arm order alternated every round, so each arm ran first five
times and this cannot bias the arm comparison.

## Design

| arm | storms | sampling | frames per seed | epochs x batches | steps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dense | 9 (`storms_train_control2.txt`) | hourly | 1013 | 51 x 32 | 1632 |
| sparse | 19 (`storms_train_expanded.txt`) | 3-hourly | 1013 | 51 x 32 | 1632 |

Training frames per band, identical in both arms and every seed; mean
label and storm count averaged over the ten draws:

| band | frames | dense mean kt | sparse mean kt | storms dense / sparse |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TD | 191 | 26.88 | 26.49 | 8 / 17 |
| TS | 371 | 46.57 | 46.02 | 9 / 19 |
| Cat 1 | 155 | 72.38 | 72.05 | 7 / 17 |
| Cat 2 | 118 | 89.11 | 88.55 | 5 / 14.7 |
| Cat 3 | 72 | 103.59 | 102.99 | 5 / 12.6 |
| Cat 4 | 80 | 124.41 | 123.50 | 5 / 10 |
| Cat 5 | 26 | 148.45 | 147.85 | 2.4 / 4 |

Each arm's frames are drawn per seed from `numpy default_rng([seed, arm])`
and recorded in `frame_lists/` with `summary.csv`, `matching.csv` and
`sha256.txt`; re-running `scripts/variety_subsets.py` reproduces them byte
for byte. 33 of 36 5 kt bins match exactly. The other three are edge slivers
holding hourly-interpolated labels that the 3-hourly pool never produces
([34, 35) TS, [83, 85) Cat 2, [113, 115) Cat 4). Their 18 dense frames have
no sparse counterpart and are dropped. Both arms: L1, unmasked, unrotated,
ResNet-18 IMAGENET1K_V1, Adam lr 1e-4, batch 32, seeds 0-9 round-robin
with the arm order alternating, checkpoint selected on
`storms_val_expanded.txt` (940 frames, none at or above 137 kt). Held-out
H9 (`storms_holdout_h9.txt`): 1752 frames, 653 above 96 kt from seven
storms. All storms are GOES-16 (2018-2024); the 2025 GOES-19 storms remain
reserved as a future cross-satellite test set.

## Limitations

- "Variety" here means more storms at lower density: the dense arm's
  frames are hourly and so more strongly autocorrelated within a storm.
  The design compares those two regimes at fixed volume; it does not vary
  storm count and temporal density independently.
- Hourly-interpolated and 3-hourly best-track labels fall differently
  within a 5 kt bin, which leaves the 0.3-0.9 kt residual label mismatch
  above. It is reported, not corrected, and works against the result.
- The dense arm is a subsample of the nine storms' 1307 frames, limited
  bin by bin by the smaller sparse pool; 18 dense frames in three orphan
  bins had no counterpart at all. Its H9 results (overall MAE 10.75) are
  close to the more-distinct-storms control on all 1307 (10.87).
- Cat 5 variety is almost the same in both arms (Dorian and Milton), and
  validation is blind at Cat 5, so this experiment says nothing about
  Cat 5.
- One validation set and one step budget.
- The three-quarters comparison with the more-distinct-storms experiment
  is descriptive, not a test.
