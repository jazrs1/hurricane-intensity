# Ensemble of the ten expanded-arm checkpoints on H9 (exploratory)

**Exploratory.** H9 has been used to evaluate several experiments, and the
expanded arm was the configuration H9 favoured, so these numbers are
optimistic for new storms. No test here is inferential. The ensemble's
real test is the 2025 GOES-19 set (design in `logs/decisions.md`,
2026-10-15).

Generated by `scripts/ensemble_eval.py` (runs the ten checkpoints and
writes `expanded10_h9.csv`, where `pred` is the unweighted mean of the ten
members' predictions and `pred_seed<s>` holds each member's) and
`scripts/ensemble_report.py` (metrics in `expanded10_h9_metrics.csv`).
Logs in `logs/ensemble/`. Members:
`models/storms/expanded/seed0-9.pt`, the validation-selected checkpoints
of the more-distinct-storms experiment. Their SHA-256s are in
`members_sha256.txt`, which defines the frozen ensemble. Each member's
predictions match the committed single-model CSVs
(`results/storms/expanded_seed<s>_h9.csv`) exactly on all 1752 frames, so
these are the checkpoints that produced those results. Metric definitions
are those of `scripts/storms_analysis.py`.

## Reading

Two things follow from arithmetic, not from the data:
- The ensemble's prediction is the members' mean, so every bias (overall,
  >96 kt, per band) equals the member mean exactly. Ensembling cannot
  reduce the high-end underestimation, and it does not.
- Per frame, the absolute error of the mean is at most the mean absolute
  error, so the ensemble's MAE cannot exceed the members' mean MAE.

What the data add: the ensemble beats every member on overall MAE, by
0.33 kt over the best member and 0.70 kt over the average. That is the
part of single-model error that comes from run-to-run variation, not from
shared bias. The gain is smallest where shared bias dominates. At Cat 5,
ensemble MAE equals the absolute bias (every Cat 5 frame is
under-predicted) and is about the same as a typical member's. At Cat 4,
11.20 against 11.83. High-end ranking improves slightly (0.601; 9 of 10
members are lower). Within Ian it does not (0.04 against a member mean of
0.07).

Per storm above 96 kt, ensemble against member mean: Kirk MAE 5.21 / 6.27,
Iota 4.74 / 6.22, Fiona 5.59 / 6.43, Lee 10.55 / 11.14, Sam 10.31 /
10.72, Ian 14.31 / 14.51, Beryl 20.99 / 21.31 (full table in
`expanded10_h9_metrics.csv`).

Don't set these numbers beside the site's 9.00 kt: that is a different
held-out set and a model trained on a different storm list.
